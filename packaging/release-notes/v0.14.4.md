## New

- Larger, consistently bold lyrics in the side panel and full-screen view, with a bright karaoke-style sweep and softer surrounding lines.
- The sweep follows wrapped lines and right-to-left text without changing layout. Clicking a timed line still seeks to it.

The sweep is a visual estimate between existing line timestamps, not precise word synchronization. Lyrics without timing, the final line without an end time, and reduced-motion mode use a steady highlight. Animation repaints only while timed lyrics are playing.
