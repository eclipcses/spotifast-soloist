## New

- **A more immersive lyrics view.** The background is now a soft field of colors from the album artwork, generated once per cover and cached at 128×128 pixels.
- **Focus on the sung line.** The current line gently grows and lifts while surrounding lines recede. Wrapping, neighboring lines and click-to-seek targets stay fixed.
- **Softer karaoke highlighting.** A feathered bright edge follows the existing real word timing, with the estimated sweep retained for line-only lyrics.
- More breathing room, a quieter full-screen header, and a smaller cover with softer corners.

Reduced-motion mode keeps the text steady. Scrolling, Follow, screen-reader labels and the queue remain available. No browser engine or continuous background animation was added.
